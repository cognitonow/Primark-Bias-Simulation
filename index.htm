Potential Biases Encountered

This exercise highlighted scenarios where common unconscious biases might influence decisions. Some types potentially encountered include:

  • Halo Effect / Horns Effect: Letting one positive or negative trait disproportionately influence overall judgment.
  • Affinity Bias: Favoring candidates who share similarities with the evaluator.
  • Stereotyping: Making assumptions based on group affiliation (e.g., education, background) instead of individual qualifications.
  • Primacy / Recency Bias: Giving undue weight to first impressions or the most recent information.
  • Central Tendency Bias: Avoiding extreme ratings and clustering candidates in the middle.
  • Educational/Experience Bias: Over-valuing specific types of degrees or experiences while potentially undervaluing others.

Awareness of these biases is the crucial first step toward making more objective and equitable decisions.
